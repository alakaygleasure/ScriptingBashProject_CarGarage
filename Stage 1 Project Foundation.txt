Project Title and description: 
This is project is based on a high end luxury Motor Dealerships records and of recording and logging imports and export cars they purchase and sell they get in such as what cars, year, fuel type, milage, how many pervious owners, km/h, brand and a section at the end of the records of details of what garage and salesperson has the car been bought from or past through. 

Intended User and Problem the application will solve: 
User will be the employees of the Business, Problem is trying to keep history of the which garages the cars have previously been with, sold out from and how many previous owners so the dealership can have a legal history of this, so this can resolve the issue of if the car is still considered newer and can be sold directly to a buyer or is the car not worth the garages resources time and money keeping and trying to sell. 

Types of records Manged by the application: 
CarReg	Brand	Year Made	How many previous owners	Milage	How many garages has it been pass down through	How long has it been with us in our garage	Info of export history	Fuel Type	Desirability on the Market	Price 

Unique Record Identifier: CarReg.

Proposed data fields and delimiter: In Folder Excel file.
Delimiter: Comma. 
Data Fields:
CarReg, CarBrand, Production Year, NoOfCurrentOwners, PreviousGarages, CurrentMilage,TimeInLot,FuelType,MarketRating,PriceEstimate

Initial Validation Rules:
CarReg is a primary key a unique identifier must not be left empty.
CarBrand cant be empty.
Production_Year Must be no older then 2027.  
NoOfOwners Must not be empty greater or equal to 1.
CurrentMilage 
PreviousGarageHistory Must contain the previous garage details or N/A if there is no previous history.
TimeInLot Cant be empty
FuelType can only be three options Petrol, Diesel, Electric.
MarketRating Cant be empty.
PriceEstimate but not be empty.

Additional rules:
When adding a new car, the system must check that the CarID does not already exist.
When updating a car, the system must check that the CarID exists.
When exporting a car, its record should be transferred to an export history file rather than permanently deleted.
Cars that have been in the garage for more than six months may receive a discount of 5% off.

Proposed Menu Options:	
1. Display All Cars
2. Search for a Car
3. Add a New Car
4. Update Car Details
5. Export / Remove a Car from Stock
6. Generate Reports
7. Backup Records
8. Exit

Please select an option:
What each option will do
Option	Purpose
Display All Cars	Displays all car records currently stored in the system.
Search for a Car	Allows an employee to search using the CarID or registration number.
Add a New Car	Adds a new car and its details to the text file.
Update Car Details	Allows employees to change information about an existing car.
Export / Remove a Car from Stock	Moves a sold or exported car into a separate history file so its record is preserved.
Generate Reports	Displays useful information about stock, older vehicles and cars requiring attention.
Backup Records	Creates a backup copy of the car records.
Exit	Closes the application.
